NP Membership

Cost: Free for non-profit organizations.

  • Listing in the member directory
  • Invitations to community networking events
  • Monthly chamber newsletter

Bronze Membership

Cost: $100 per year.

  • Everything in NP Membership
  • Discounted tickets to chamber events
  • Access to quarterly training workshops

Silver Membership

Cost: $250 per year.

  • Everything in Bronze Membership
  • Advertising space on the chamber website
  • Priority booking for event sponsorships

Gold Membership

Cost: $500 per year.

  • Everything in Silver Membership
  • Spotlight position on the home page
  • Free admission to all chamber events
  • Featured business profile in the newsletter
